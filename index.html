Privacy Policy

How the EarnRise mobile application handles information.

Effective date: October 13, 2026 · Last updated: October 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the EarnRise mobile application (“EarnRise,” “we,” “our,” or “the app”) handles information when you use it. Please read it before using the app.

Important: This policy describes the app version reviewed for publication. If the app is later connected to online services, analytics, advertising, payment processors, or other third parties, this policy and the app’s Google Play Data safety answers must be updated before that version is released.

1. Information You Enter

Depending on the features you use, the app may let you enter or create the following information:

  • Account details, including your name and email address.
  • Account credentials. The app stores a password-derived value in its local app database; do not reuse a password that you use for another service.
  • Referral codes and app activity, such as tasks completed, referral records, displayed balances, and transaction or investment records.
  • Deposit or withdrawal request details, including amounts, payment method details, and a cryptocurrency wallet address you enter.
  • Information you choose to include in a request or other app input.

2. Where Information Is Stored

In the reviewed version, account and activity records are stored in a database on your device. The app manifest and reviewed source code did not indicate a configured developer-operated account server or a permission for general internet access. Accordingly, this version is not represented as providing verified online account synchronization or actual blockchain payment processing. Figures and request statuses shown in the app should not be treated as proof that a payment or investment transaction occurred.

Information stored on your device may be lost if you clear app data, uninstall the app, lose the device, or reset the device. Device-level backup settings may also affect app data. Keep your device secured and do not share access to it.

3. How Information Is Used

The app uses information you enter to provide its on-device features, including account screens, task and referral records, displayed balances, and deposit or withdrawal request screens. Information is not described in this policy as being sold or shared with the developer through an online backend in the reviewed version.

4. Third-Party Services and Links

The app may display links to third-party content, including YouTube videos. If you open a third-party service, that service may collect information under its own privacy policy and terms. We do not control third-party services. Review the relevant provider’s privacy policy before using it.

5. Data Sharing and Sale

In the reviewed version, we do not operate a configured backend that receives the information entered into the app, and we do not sell personal information. If a future version introduces server connectivity, advertising, analytics, payment processing, or other data sharing, this section will be revised to explain those practices.

6. Security

We take a cautious approach to the information handled by the app, but no device or software can be guaranteed completely secure. Use a strong device passcode, keep your operating system updated, and avoid entering sensitive information on a device you do not control. Do not enter wallet recovery phrases, private keys, or exchange passwords into EarnRise.

7. Children’s Privacy

EarnRise is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us using the details below.

8. Your Choices and Deleting Information

You can stop using the app at any time. Because the reviewed version stores its records locally, you can generally remove those records by clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling the app. This may permanently delete local records. Any copies retained by your device’s backup system are controlled by your device and backup settings.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app’s features or data practices change. The latest version should be published at the same public URL, with the updated date shown above. Please review it periodically.
